Introduction & Overview

Chicku Run is provided as a Free-to-Play mobile application by Nusrat Shaheen Apps Suite. This document informs players and parents regarding our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our game.

By downloading and playing Chicku Run, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this policy.

2

Information Collection & Direct Usage

Chicku Run DOES NOT directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as real names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or contact lists on private external servers.

All core game mechanics, stage grids, profile scores, virtual coins, and game settings run locally on your Android device.

3

Google Play Games Services (Leaderboards & Achievements)

Chicku Run integrates Google Play Games Services to provide competitive global leaderboards and score achievements. When you sign in or interact with Google Play Games in Chicku Run:

  • ✓ Your public Gamer ID, avatar, and high score statistics are synchronized with Google Play Games servers.
  • ✓ High scores are displayed on public leaderboards to allow competitive ranking among friends and global players.
  • ✓ We do not store your Google account credentials or passwords on any servers owned by Nusrat Shaheen Apps Suite.
Control & Opt-out: You can manage, disconnect, or request deletion of your Play Games profile and leaderboard data at any time via your Android device's Google Play Games app settings.
4

Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep Chicku Run free for all players, the game integrates trusted third-party SDKs (such as Google Play Services, Google Play Games, and Google AdMob) to serve non-intrusive, family-friendly advertisements and manage basic telemetry.

These third-party SDKs may automatically process anonymous, non-sensitive technical parameters including:

🔹 Anonymous Advertising IDs (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
🔹 Device model, OS version, and language settings
🔹 Coarse geographic region (Country/City level)
🔹 Game performance diagnostics and crash logs
Opt-out Notice: You can reset or disable personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
5

Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy Compliance

Chicku Run is designed to be a family-friendly arcade and educational quiz game suitable for players of all ages, including children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy requirements.

  • ★ We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • ★ Advertisements served within Chicku Run comply with Google Play Families ad policies and strictly adhere to appropriate content rating guidelines.
  • ★ If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such data.
6

Data Retention, Storage & User Deletion Rights

📱 Local Game Data

Offline stage progress, virtual coin balances, and local profiles remain stored strictly on your local device storage. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in Android settings.

☁️ Cloud / Play Games Data

Leaderboards and achievements synced via Google Play Games can be cleared or managed directly through the Google Play Games profile settings.

7

Security & Policy Updates

We value your trust in downloading Chicku Run and employ industry-standard safety practices. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards or game features. You are advised to review this policy occasionally. Changes become effective immediately upon being published on this page.
